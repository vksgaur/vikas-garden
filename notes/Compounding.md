Compounding is the process by which something grows exponentially because each increment of growth is added to the base, which then grows further.

## In Finance

₹1 lakh invested at 15% annual return becomes ₹1 crore in about 33 years. The growth looks slow for the first decade and explosive in the last decade. Most people give up during the slow part.

## In Knowledge

[[The Scientific Method]] produces compounding knowledge. Newton built on Galileo. Darwin built on geology. Einstein built on Newton. Each generation starts from a higher base.

This is why [[The Enlightenment]] mattered so much — it didn't just produce individual geniuses. It built a _system_ for compounding intellectual progress.

## In Institutions

Good [[Institutions Matter More Than Geography|institutions]] compound too. Inclusive political institutions → better economic policies → prosperity → stronger institutions → even better policies. This virtuous cycle is what separates rich nations from poor ones.

## The Key Insight

> The first rule of compounding: never interrupt it unnecessarily. — Charlie Munger

This applies to money, knowledge, relationships, and institutional trust. The most catastrophic thing a society can do is break the chain of compounding.
